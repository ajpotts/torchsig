Title: Add opt-in structured HDF5 materialization to TorchSig DataModules

Summary
TorchSig's structured HDF5 path can precompute deterministic, model-ready
samples, but users currently have to manage conversion and dataset selection
outside the Lightning DataModules. Add an explicit opt-in integration for both
TorchSigDataModule and SplitTorchSigDataModule without changing their existing
default behavior.

Requirements
- Allow both DataModules to materialize structured HDF5 datasets during
  prepare_data and select them during setup.
- Keep the current Signal-oriented dataset path as the default.
- Require separate source and structured output roots.
- Preserve seeded logical splits for TorchSigDataModule and physical
  train/validation/test separation for SplitTorchSigDataModule.
- Store compatibility manifests and reuse only outputs whose source,
  configuration, layout, labels, and expected length still match.
- Reject stale or incompatible outputs unless overwrite is explicitly enabled.
- Expose materialization batch size, worker count, validation, and writer
  options.
- Keep explicitly supplied randomized training transforms online instead of
  freezing them into the materialized data.
- Support zero-worker and multi-worker DataLoaders.

Acceptance criteria
- Existing DataModule construction and loading are unchanged when structured
  materialization is disabled.
- Compatible structured outputs are reused; mismatch and overwrite behavior is
  covered by deterministic tests.
- Single-dataset and split-dataset layouts load the expected samples and
  preserve their split semantics.
- Online training transforms apply only to the training dataset.
- User documentation includes an end-to-end DataModule example and explains
  manifests, output roots, and overwrite behavior.
