Title: Validate wideband boundary placement and localization labels after bandwidth measurement

Summary
-------
Wideband component placement and YOLO labels consume the final component
``center_freq`` and ``bandwidth``. Once bandwidth measurement semantics are
corrected, the full pipeline needs deterministic integration coverage proving
that boundary clipping leaves consistent metadata and that localization labels
describe the final visible component extent.

This ticket follows ``wideband_frequency_metadata_ticket.txt``. It should use
the bandwidth behavior established there rather than defining a second
bandwidth policy.

Current state
-------------
- ``lower_freq`` and ``upper_freq`` are derived from canonical ``center_freq``
  and ``bandwidth`` values.
- Updating either canonical field invalidates legacy cached edge values.
- ``frequency_shift_signal`` updates center frequency and bandwidth after its
  anti-aliasing filter.
- ``YOLOLabel`` derives vertical center and height from ``center_freq`` and
  ``bandwidth``.

These units have focused tests, but the reported wideband failure does not yet
have an end-to-end regression covering measurement, placement at both dataset
boundaries, and the resulting localization label.

Requested work
--------------
1. Add deterministic integration tests that pass protocol and constellation
   components through bandwidth measurement and wideband frequency placement.
2. Exercise clipping at both the lower and upper dataset frequency limits.
3. Verify final ``lower_freq`` and ``upper_freq`` equal
   ``center_freq - bandwidth / 2`` and ``center_freq + bandwidth / 2``.
4. Verify final edges remain within the configured dataset frequency range.
5. Apply ``YOLOLabel`` and verify its vertical center and height match the final
   canonical frequency metadata.

Acceptance criteria
-------------------
- Tests cover an 802.11a waveform and a constellation waveform.
- Lower-bound and upper-bound clipping are both covered deterministically.
- Final frequency edges remain within dataset bounds within numerical
  tolerance.
- No ``_lower_frequency`` or ``_upper_frequency`` value can override final
  canonical metadata.
- YOLO vertical geometry matches the final visible frequency interval and
  remains within normalized image bounds.

Out of scope
------------
- Choosing or implementing the bandwidth estimator; that work belongs to
  ``wideband_frequency_metadata_ticket.txt``.
- Changes to the metadata debug logger.
