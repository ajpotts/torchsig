Title: Support variable-length WAV and OGG records with an explicit audio manifest

TorchSIG's WAV and OGG readers currently derive each record location from a
uniform ``elements_per_file`` and ``num_iq_samples`` layout. That assumption
prevents datasets from storing variable-length records or uneven numbers of
records per audio file, and it makes metadata incapable of identifying the
actual waveform segment associated with a row.

Add a shared audio-record layout abstraction used by both readers. Header-based
``metadata.csv`` rows should be able to declare a relative ``file_path``,
``start_frame``, and ``num_frames``, with optional ``expected_sample_rate`` and
``channel_count`` values. Resolve and validate every record when the reader is
constructed: paths must remain inside the dataset root, offsets and lengths
must be nonnegative, segments must fit inside their files, and records in the
same file must not overlap.

Preserve compatibility with existing fixed-length datasets by adapting
``num_iq_samples`` and ``elements_per_file`` into the same internal descriptor
model. NPY, partial reads, handle caching, codec/fidelity enforcement, and
normalization are outside this ticket.

Acceptance criteria:

- WAV and OGG share one internal record-layout implementation.
- Variable-length records and uneven per-file record counts are supported.
- Nested relative audio paths are supported without allowing root traversal.
- Missing, invalid, overlapping, and truncated record descriptors fail during
  reader construction with actionable errors.
- Every metadata row maps to exactly one valid audio segment.
- Existing fixed-length WAV and OGG datasets remain readable.
- Equivalent WAV and OGG manifests produce equivalent record indexes.
