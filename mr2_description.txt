Summary

Adds StructuredHDF5Writer and StructuredHDF5Reader as a separate model-ready storage API. Structured samples are described by the MR1 schema and stored as one extensible HDF5 dataset per numeric leaf. Files carry a JSON schema plus format, version, length, and completion metadata, and readers validate the complete physical layout before exposing data.

The writer infers its schema from the first sample or accepts an explicit schema for zero-length datasets. It validates every later sample before appending a batch. The reader reconstructs mapping, tuple, and list containers for single samples and performs one contiguous slice per leaf for batch reads. Existing HomogeneousHDF5Reader and HomogeneousHDF5Writer behavior and their version 1 format are unchanged.

Benchmark assessment

No benchmark is required for MR2. This change establishes correctness and the physical storage API, but it does not yet provide the Dataset/DataLoader materialization path or worker-safe lifecycle needed for representative throughput comparisons. The merge plan assigns the benchmark to MR7, after MRs 3 through 6. At that point, benchmark online deterministic transforms against materialized structured reads with zero and multiple workers, reporting generation time, epoch throughput, and break-even epoch count. A small ad hoc read benchmark may be run during review if a performance regression is suspected, but it is not an MR2 acceptance gate.
