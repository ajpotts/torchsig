MR12 benchmark results

The quick synthetic IQ benchmark strongly favors structured materialization.
The recommended tested configuration was uncompressed HDF5 with one sample per
chunk, two DataLoader workers, and the full-dataset layout.

For shuffled access with two workers, throughput increased from 3,729.8
samples/s online to 22,609.1 samples/s materialized, a 6.06x end-to-end
speedup. This comfortably exceeds the predeclared 1.50x throughput target.
Materialization reached 46.5% of the 48,593.4 samples/s device-only ceiling and
is estimated to pay for itself after 2.04 epochs. The structured copy added
approximately 0.01 GiB for this test.

Worker behavior depended on access pattern. Two workers improved shuffled
materialized throughput from 17,862.9 to 22,609.1 samples/s, while sequential
materialized access was faster with zero workers: 24,520.7 versus 19,920.8
samples/s. The fair shuffled comparison uses matching worker counts; the 6.06x
two-worker result is therefore the relevant headline rather than the 11.00x
zero-worker result.

This was a short directional run using one repetition, a small synthetic IQ
dataset, and only the full layout. Repetition stability is not established and
physical split separation was not measured. The result supports proceeding
with the uncompressed, one-sample-chunk, two-worker configuration, but a final
production recommendation should be confirmed with at least three repetitions
and the representative production transform and model factories.
