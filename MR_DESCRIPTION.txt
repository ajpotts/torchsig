Title: Make constellation pulse shaping configurable

Summary:
- Allow constellation signal generators to use a fixed `pulse_shape_name` of
  `srrc` or `rectangular`.
- Allow callers to fix the SRRC `alpha_rolloff`, while retaining the existing
  `alpha` configuration alias.
- Preserve randomized pulse-shape and rolloff selection when neither setting is
  supplied.
- Validate incompatible, conflicting, and out-of-range settings.

Testing:
- `pytest tests/signals/builders/test_constellation.py`
