Title: Preserve sampling-clock output length and complex64 dtype

Target branch: 3.0.0-dev

Summary

This MR makes clock drift and jitter reliably preserve their input sample count
and complex64 output contract.

- Cover shorter, equal, and odd/even longer resampler outputs.
- Add the corresponding focused sub-ticket.
