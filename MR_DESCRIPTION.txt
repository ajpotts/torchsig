## Summary

Define and test the occupied-bandwidth contract of the sampling-clock interpolation filter.

## Dependency and merge order

This branch targets `414-replace-symmetric-clock-output-padding` and is merge-order step 5. See `tickets/drift_and_jitter/MERGE_ORDER.md`.

## Changes

- Define a maximum full two-sided occupied bandwidth of 0.7 times sample rate.
- Document the corresponding ±0.35 passband edges on both clock functionals.
- Add response tests requiring less than 0.05 dB edge loss and at least 80 dB rejection by 0.7 cycles per input sample.
- Make the bandwidth constant available to diagnostics and callers.

## Testing

- Prototype passband-edge response
- Image-region rejection
- Full-bandwidth versus one-sided-edge convention
