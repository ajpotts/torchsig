Summary

Corrects fractional polyphase resampling for requested rates near unity by
selecting the closest available integer up/down ratio.

Changes

- Adds _polyphase_fractional_integer_rates to centralize conversion of a
  floating-point fractional rate into integer up/down rates.
- Replaces ceil-based down-rate selection with rounding to the nearest integer.
- Clamps the selected down rate to a minimum of one.
- Adds tests covering near-unity rate accuracy.
- Adds tests confirming symmetric quantization errors around a 1:1 rate.

Rationale

The previous ceil-based calculation always biased the effective resampling rate
in the same direction. For small Doppler shifts, especially rates very close to
unity, this could turn a small requested shift into a noticeably larger one and
produce asymmetric behavior for approaching and receding velocities.

This is the first MR in the Doppler correctness and performance series. It is a
focused DSP correctness change and does not include filter caching, dtype
optimization, scalar Doppler changes, batched Doppler support, or benchmarks.
