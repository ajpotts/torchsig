Title: Keep sampling-clock NumPy and Numba behavior deterministic and equivalent

Summary

The NumPy and Numba sampling-clock implementations must produce deterministic,
numerically equivalent results for the same inputs, parameters, and random
seed.

Scope

- Add deterministic parity tests covering sampling-clock drift and jitter.
- Document and enforce the numerical tolerance used to compare both paths.

Acceptance criteria

- Repeated calls with identical inputs, parameters, and seeds produce identical
  results within each implementation.
- NumPy and Numba outputs agree within an absolute tolerance of 1e-4.
- Tests cover drift-only, jitter-only, and combined drift-and-jitter cases.

Out of scope

Sampling-clock API changes, new impairment semantics, and performance work.
