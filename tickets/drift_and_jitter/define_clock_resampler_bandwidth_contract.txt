Title: Define and validate the sampling-clock resampler bandwidth contract

Summary

The clock resampler's prototype filter is suitable for oversampled signals but
is not transparent for signals occupying most of Nyquist. The audited design
showed measurable band-edge attenuation near 0.45 cycles per sample and weak
image rejection immediately beyond Nyquist relative to the nominal 120 dB
stopband target. Float32 coefficient storage further limits deep-stopband
fidelity.

Without a documented occupancy limit, users can attribute filter-induced
shaping or image leakage to the requested clock impairment.

Proposed change

Define the maximum supported occupied bandwidth and required passband/image
rejection for sampling-clock transforms. Then either redesign the prototype to
meet that contract or validate and document the existing design's supported
region. Evaluate whether filter design or accumulation should use higher
precision before casting final IQ output to TorchSIG's complex dtype.

Acceptance criteria

- The supported normalized occupied-bandwidth range is documented.
- Automated response tests cover passband ripple, transition-band attenuation,
  and image rejection in input-frequency units.
- Tests distinguish filter artifacts from clock-impairment effects.
- The relationship between the 120 dB design target and float32 coefficients
  is measured and documented.
- Any prototype redesign includes output-alignment and performance regression
  coverage.
