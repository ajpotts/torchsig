Title: Correct and expand sampling-clock transform documentation

Summary

The `clock_drift` and `clock_jitter` docstrings describe the effect as Local
Oscillator drift and return "LO drift" data. These transforms perturb ADC
sampling time, not carrier LO phase or frequency. The documentation also does
not state the drift sign convention, the distinction between static clock
offset and time-varying drift, or the fact that ADC-clock and carrier-offset
effects are modeled independently.

Proposed change

Rewrite the functional and transform documentation to describe sampling-clock
timing behavior precisely. Document:

- the sign convention for positive and negative sampling-rate offset;
- whether metadata remains nominal after the transform;
- the distinction between ADC timing error and carrier LO error;
- the independence of clock and carrier impairments in TorchSIG;
- the meaning and units of initial phase and jitter parameters;
- the occupied-bandwidth assumptions imposed by the interpolation filter.

Acceptance criteria

- No sampling-clock API describes its result as LO drift.
- Positive and negative drift examples demonstrate the documented sign.
- Static offset, independent jitter, accumulated period error, and correlated
  jitter terminology are used consistently where applicable.
- Functional APIs, transform classes, and user-facing documentation agree.
- Documentation states whether frequency and sample-rate metadata are nominal
  or adjusted after impairment.
