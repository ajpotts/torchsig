Title: Document frequency metadata semantics after sampling-clock impairments

Summary

`ClockDrift` and `ClockJitter` modify sample timing but leave `center_freq`,
`bandwidth`, and `sample_rate` metadata unchanged. For time-varying drift or
jitter, there may be no single exact center frequency or bandwidth that
describes the impaired data, so retaining nominal metadata can be appropriate.
The current behavior is not documented, however, and consumers may assume that
frequency metadata describes the measured post-impairment spectrum exactly.

Proposed change

- Document that frequency and sample-rate metadata remain nominal after these
  impairments.
- Determine whether transforms with a constant sample-rate offset should update
  metadata or add impairment parameters that allow consumers to derive apparent
  values.
- Do not attempt to replace time-varying behavior with a misleading single
  corrected center frequency.

Acceptance criteria

- Public transform documentation states whether `center_freq`, `bandwidth`, and
  `sample_rate` are nominal or measured after impairment.
- Tests explicitly verify the selected metadata behavior.
- If impairment parameters are exposed in metadata, their units and sign
  convention are documented and deterministic tests validate them.
