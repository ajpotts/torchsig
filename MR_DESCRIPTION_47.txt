## Summary

- expand generic OFDM signal classes to every subcarrier count from 4 through 8192, inclusive
- keep `num_subcarrier_values` available as a list while deriving it and the public signal registry from one shared range
- support odd subcarrier counts by distributing every active carrier around the DC notch
- use a valid one-sample cyclic-prefix lower bound for four- and five-carrier OFDM signals
- add coverage for the full registry range and odd-carrier frequency-bin placement
