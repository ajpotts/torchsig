Title: Skip out-of-range impaired sample positions

Problem

Sampling-clock impairments silently clip sample positions to the valid input
range. When an impairment remains outside that range, the resampler repeatedly
reads the same boundary position and creates artificial constant-value output
segments.

Scope

- Detect impaired sample positions outside the valid range.
- Skip those positions instead of clipping them to a boundary.
- Continue advancing the clock state so later positions can re-enter the valid
  range.
- Apply the behavior consistently in NumPy and Numba.

Acceptance criteria

- Out-of-range positions do not produce output samples.
- Boundary samples are not repeated as a result of clipping.
- Clock state continues advancing after a skipped position.
- NumPy and Numba produce matching results.

Out of scope

Changes to the jitter or drift models, RNG distributions, or public APIs.
