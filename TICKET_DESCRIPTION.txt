Title: Validate and clarify sampling-clock engine inputs

Problem

The NumPy and Numba sampling-clock entry points currently rely on downstream
arithmetic and random-number calls to reject invalid parameters. This produces
indirect errors and fails when the optional random-number generator is omitted.
Several internal position and boundary names are also ambiguous.

Scope

- Require a positive integer polyphase up-rate.
- Require a finite, positive down-rate.
- Require finite, nonnegative jitter and finite drift values.
- Reject drift values whose effective nominal increment is nonfinite or
  nonpositive.
- Create a default NumPy generator when `rng` is omitted.
- Rename internal input-boundary variables for clarity.

Acceptance criteria

- NumPy and Numba entry paths raise the same explicit `ValueError` messages.
- Valid calls with `rng=None` complete successfully.
- Validation does not change valid sampling-clock behavior.

Out of scope

Changes to drift, jitter, output-length, or boundary-clamping semantics.
