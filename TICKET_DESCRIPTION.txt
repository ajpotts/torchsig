Title: Add comprehensive sampling-clock engine regressions

Problem

The corrected engine contract needs explicit regression coverage for invalid
parameters, buffer capacity, RNG usage, dtype preservation, boundary clipping,
and empty or short inputs in both NumPy and Numba implementations.

Scope and acceptance criteria

- Validate uprate, drate, jitter, drift, and positive position increments.
- Verify jitter is scaled in input-sample-period units.
- Verify no RNG samples are consumed when no random impairment is requested.
- Cover real-valued complex input, empty input, and one-sample input.
- Cover negative jitter clipping and raw output-length behavior.
- Verify defensive output-capacity failures and normal capacity estimates.
- Preserve complex64 output and finite values.
- Confirm NumPy/Numba parity for all covered cases.

Dependency

Depends on 165-clock-engine-semantics.
