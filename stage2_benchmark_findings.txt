Stage-2 Materialization Benchmark Findings
==========================================

Objective
---------
Determine whether precomputing deterministic Stage-2 transforms into structured
HDF5 improves training throughput enough to justify the additional storage and
one-time materialization cost.

Test setup
----------
- 100,000 deterministic synthetic IQ samples
- IQ length: 4,096
- Batch size: 64
- FP32 training
- Built-in fallback Stage-2 transform and model pipeline
- Online, materialized, and device-only pipelines compared

Exploratory results
-------------------
The shortened configuration sweep compared 0 and 4 DataLoader workers,
uncompressed and LZF storage, and chunk sizes of 1 and 32. The strongest
shuffled configuration was:

- Compression: none
- Chunk size: 1 sample
- DataLoader workers: 4
- Layout: full dataset
- Materialized throughput: 17,746 samples/second
- Speedup over the matching online pipeline: 4.26x
- Device-only ceiling utilization: 59.3%
- Additional storage: 3.12 GiB
- Estimated break-even: 3.23 epochs

The exploratory run used one repetition, so it identified a promising
configuration but did not establish measurement stability. It also showed that
LZF compression and 32-sample chunks substantially reduced throughput.

Confirmation results
--------------------
The winning configuration was measured over three repetitions with 10 warmup
steps and 100 benchmark steps per repetition.

Shuffled access:
- Online throughput: 5,556 samples/second
- Materialized throughput: 19,356 samples/second
- Speedup: 3.48x
- Device-only ceiling: 30,952 samples/second
- Device-only ceiling utilization: 62.5%
- Estimated break-even: 4.63 epochs

Sequential access:
- Online throughput: 5,837 samples/second
- Materialized throughput: 16,642 samples/second
- Speedup: 2.85x

The confirmation run met the benchmark's stability criterion of no more than
5% standard deviation across repetitions.

Conclusion
----------
Precomputing deterministic Stage-2 transforms is beneficial for training runs
lasting approximately five epochs or longer. The recommended configuration is
uncompressed structured HDF5 with one sample per chunk, four DataLoader workers,
and the full-dataset layout. This configuration provides a stable 3.48x shuffled
training speedup at a storage cost of 3.12 GiB.

The materialized pipeline reaches 62.5% of the device-only ceiling, indicating
that additional input-pipeline optimization may still be possible. Physical
train/validation/test split separation was not measured. Results use the
benchmark's synthetic data and built-in fallback transform/model, so the final
production decision should be validated with the real Stage-2 pipeline and
representative hardware when those are available.
